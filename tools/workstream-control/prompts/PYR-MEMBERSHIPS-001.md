## Task: Memberships, Associations & Events

Identify the associations, trade bodies, conferences, memberships and events that would give Pyrnova real leverage: customer access, useful intelligence, partnerships, or institutional credibility.

Do this:

1. **Value lens.** For anything you propose, state which benefit it delivers — customer access, market/competitive intelligence, partnership surface, or credibility — and how strongly. Reject options that only signal busyness.

2. **Candidates.** Using current web research, list relevant bodies and events across Pyrnova's markets (defense and any prioritised commercial adjacents). For each: what it is, who attends/belongs, cost and commitment, access it unlocks, and the realistic payoff for a small near-launch company.

3. **Credibility vs cost.** Distinguish memberships that confer genuine institutional credibility from expensive vanity. Note any that gate access to buyers or programs Pyrnova will eventually need.

4. **Sequenced plan.** Recommend a short, prioritised calendar/shortlist: what to join or attend first, what to watch, and what to skip for now — with rough cost and expected return.

5. **Privacy fit.** Flag where founder privacy doctrine affects participation (attend vs speak vs sponsor, named vs company presence).

Keep it decision-ready: a ranked shortlist with costs and payoffs, not an exhaustive directory. Cite sources and note dates for events.

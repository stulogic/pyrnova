## Task: Commercial Market Expansion

Identify valuable Pyrnova applications outside defense. Concentrate on markets where external change creates expensive decisions *before* conventional information environments explain the consequence — Pyrnova's core edge.

Do this:

1. **Screening lens.** Define the qualities that make a market a strong fit: high cost of being late, consequential decisions triggered by external events, weak or slow incumbent sensing, buyers who will pay for lead time, and reachable go-to-market. Use this lens consistently.

2. **Candidate markets.** Using current web research, generate and screen candidate sectors (e.g. finance/risk, insurance, supply chain and commodities, critical infrastructure and utilities, corporate security and geopolitical risk, pharma/regulatory, energy). For each surviving candidate: the expensive decision, the triggering external change, who owns that decision, what they use today, and why they'd be late without Pyrnova.

3. **Rank.** Score candidates on fit, reachability, and revenue potential. Recommend a shortlist of 2–4 worth real exploration and say which single beachhead you'd test first and why.

4. **Adjacency cost.** For the top picks, note what (if anything) Pyrnova would need to add or reframe to serve them, and whether that pulls against defense focus or complements it.

Be selective and decisive. A ranked shortlist with reasoning beats a broad survey. Cite sources for market facts.

## Task: Current Product Feature & Competitive Roadmap

Benchmark near-launch Pyrnova against the competitors and adjacents that a serious buyer would actually evaluate it beside. Produce a roadmap view that sharpens Phase 1 without inflating it.

Do this:

1. **Frame the comparison set.** Identify the direct competitors and the adjacent tools a target customer might substitute or combine. State briefly, for each, what job it does and where it wins.

2. **Capability matrix.** Compare Pyrnova to that set across the dimensions that drive a purchase decision: signal quality and lead time, source breadth, explainability/provenance, alerting and workflow fit, integration, trust/security posture, and pricing model. Mark where Pyrnova leads, matches, or trails. Use current web research for competitor facts and cite them.

3. **Consequential gaps.** Isolate the small number of gaps that would actually lose or win deals at Phase 1 — separate these clearly from "nice to have" and from things safe to defer.

4. **Recommendations.** For each consequential gap, recommend a specific addition or sharpening, its customer-decision value, rough build weight, and whether it belongs in Phase 1 or a named later phase. Explicitly flag anything you would *cut* to protect focus.

Prioritise decisions the founder can act on. Avoid feature lists for their own sake; every recommendation must tie to a customer decision Pyrnova makes cheaper, faster, or earlier.

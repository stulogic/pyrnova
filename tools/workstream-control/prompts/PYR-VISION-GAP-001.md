## Task: Original Vision vs Current Pyrnova

Compare Pyrnova's original concept, research and ideation with the product as it stands today, and produce an honest reconciliation of what happened to the original ambition.

Do this:

1. **Reconstruct the original vision.** From existing Pyrnova authority, early research and ideation material, extract the founding ambitions, principles, and intended capabilities. State them as a concise list of discrete claims, each traceable to source.

2. **Classify each ambition** against today's product into exactly one bucket:
   - **Achieved** — present and working as intended.
   - **Deliberately deferred** — consciously postponed, with the reasoning.
   - **Accidentally lost** — dropped without a deliberate decision (drift).
   - **Obsolete** — no longer worth doing; say why.
   - **Worth restoring** — dropped or deferred but still valuable; make the case.

3. **Focus on the consequential.** Highlight where drift has quietly changed what Pyrnova *is*, and where a small correction would recover disproportionate value.

4. **Recommendations.** For "worth restoring" items, give a crisp restoration rationale and rough cost. For "accidentally lost" items, note whether to formally accept the loss or reverse it.

Be candid about drift; the value of this workstream is catching silent divergence between intent and reality. Do not reopen decisions explicitly marked closed — flag them instead.

## Shared Pyrnova context

Pyrnova is a decision-intelligence product approaching Phase 1 launch. It exists to surface consequential external change early — before conventional information environments have explained the consequence — so customers can act on expensive decisions ahead of the field.

Ground your work in existing Pyrnova project authority and prior handovers rather than re-deriving the concept. Where contemporary facts matter (competitors, markets, tools, events, regulations), use current web research and cite concrete sources with dates.

Research produced in this workstream is advisory. It does **not** automatically modify Phase 1 authority and does not reopen decisions explicitly marked closed. Anything that ought to change authority must be surfaced explicitly under MASTER HANDOVER UPDATES for human review, not assumed. Prefer recommendations that raise customer decision value without bloating Phase 1 scope.

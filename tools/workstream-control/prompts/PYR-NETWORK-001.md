## Task: Founder & Corporate Network Development

Design a practical professional networking system for Pyrnova that identifies and organises the relationships worth building — customers, allies, experts, introducers, advisers, and mutually beneficial non-competing partners — while respecting founder privacy doctrine.

Do this:

1. **Relationship taxonomy.** Define the categories of people/organisations worth tracking and what each is *for* (e.g. buyers, champions, domain experts, warm introducers, advisers, complementary vendors, community/credibility nodes). Keep it small and usable.

2. **Target identification.** Using current web research where useful, propose how to find high-value targets in each category relevant to Pyrnova's markets, and give concrete example profiles/roles (not a mass list — representative, high-signal examples).

3. **Lightweight system.** Recommend a simple, low-overhead way to track relationships, stages, and next actions that fits a solo/small founder (fields, stages, cadence). Bias to the least tooling that works; avoid CRM sprawl.

4. **Engagement doctrine.** Give principles for outreach and reciprocity — how to offer value first, how to sequence asks, and how to preserve founder privacy doctrine (what is and isn't disclosed publicly, use of proxies/company voice).

5. **First moves.** Recommend the first handful of concrete relationships to pursue and why.

Output should be usable as an operating playbook, not a theory of networking. Cite sources for any named external facts.

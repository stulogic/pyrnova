## Task: Social & Market Monitoring System

Design a monitoring system that watches competitors, prospects, keywords, buying signals, relevant discussions, and opportunities for useful engagement — feeding Pyrnova's own market awareness.

Do this:

1. **Watch targets.** Define what to monitor and why, in tiers: competitors (products, hiring, funding, messaging), prospects and their triggering events, topical keywords and narratives, and buying/intent signals. Keep the target set focused on things that change a decision.

2. **Signal definitions.** For each target type, specify the concrete signals worth catching and what they imply (e.g. a competitor hire, a prospect's leadership change, a regulatory shift, a relevant public discussion). Distinguish noise from signal.

3. **Method & tooling.** Using current web research, recommend a pragmatic, low-overhead way to collect these — native platform tools, alerts, feeds, lightweight aggregation — favouring the least tooling that works and standard, well-documented services only. Note where paid tools genuinely earn their place.

4. **Triage & action.** Define how signals are reviewed (cadence, ownership) and turned into action: when to log, when to engage publicly, when to route to sales/network. Include rules for *useful* engagement that fit founder privacy doctrine.

5. **Dogfood note.** Flag where Pyrnova's own product could or should eventually serve this need internally.

Deliver an operable monitoring playbook a solo founder can run, not an enterprise SOC design. Cite tool facts and pricing where relevant.

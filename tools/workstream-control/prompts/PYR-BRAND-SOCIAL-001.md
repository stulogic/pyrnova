## Task: Social Brand Asset System

Define Pyrnova's social presence and the asset system behind it, while preserving founder privacy doctrine.

Do this:

1. **Presence strategy.** Recommend which platforms Pyrnova should hold or actively use (and which to reserve but not run), matched to where its buyers and credibility actually live. Justify each; avoid presence for its own sake.

2. **Company voice & copy.** Provide the core copy kit: one-line descriptor, short bio/boilerplate (a couple of lengths), tone/voice guidelines, and do/don't language. Keep it consistent with Pyrnova's positioning as early-warning decision intelligence.

3. **Visual assets.** Specify the asset set and requirements: avatar/logomark usage, header/banner dimensions per platform, colour and type direction (reference existing brand authority rather than inventing new identity), and a checklist of files/sizes needed to stand up profiles.

4. **Founder privacy doctrine.** Make explicit what is presented as company vs founder, what personal detail is never published, and how to run credible social presence without exposing the founder. Give concrete rules.

5. **Profile build sheet.** A per-platform checklist Stu can execute: handle, display name, bio, links, image assets, and any verification steps.

Reference and stay consistent with existing Pyrnova brand authority; do not redesign the brand. Cite current platform spec facts (image sizes, features) where they matter.

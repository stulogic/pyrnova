## Core findings

- Open and authoritative public data gets Pyrnova **much further than initially assumed**.
- Institutional-quality public-company and government-contractor intelligence is **feasible** from
  open/authoritative sources.
- Public/open evidence supports **strong procurement, policy, and capital intelligence**.
- **Dataminr-equivalent global real-time detection is not economically sensible initially.**
- **Complete global supply-chain reconstruction is not feasible from public data alone.**
- Proprietary premium document corpora **require licensing** (rights obligations attach).
- Canonical identity, bitemporality, assertions, immutable provenance, rights metadata, and read
  projections **matter** and are the foundation of the intelligence layer.
- **Own the intelligence layer; selectively rent evidence that cannot economically be recreated.**

## Resulting decisions

- Informs D-045 (Phase 1 wedge is feasible from open/authoritative data), D-048 (global detection and
  broad supply-chain reconstruction deferred), D-049 (the intelligence layer, not the raw data, is the
  candidate moat), and the architectural principles in `01-PROJECT-AUTHORITY.md` (canonical identity,
  bitemporality, provenance, rights metadata, read projections).
- Reinforces the global source-ingestion rule (D-008): archive once, replay many; rent evidence
  sparingly under explicit rights isolation.

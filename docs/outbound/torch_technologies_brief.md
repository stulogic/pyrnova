- **Evidence:** https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/0cd637419e794be7bee14a0867762722/view

  incumbents.
- **Next move:** submit an RFI response positioning your MW/T modeling & test support; engage SSC/SNK;
  line up prime teaming before the solicitation.

- **Why it's yours:** ground-segment systems engineering, integration and T&E for missile

- **Where you actually fit (straight):** ground SE&I / integration / test or subcontract to the ground

  likely integrator primes to team with.

## 1. EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY

Pyrnova is a customer-specific external-change intelligence product.

Its purpose is:

**Identify economically meaningful external change for a specific organization, determine why it matters, support the conclusion with auditable evidence and temporal truth, support investigation and action, and learn from the result.**

The operating loop is:

**EXTERNAL CHANGE**
→ **CUSTOMER RELEVANCE**
→ **COMMERCIAL CONSEQUENCE**
→ **EVIDENCE**
→ **INVESTIGATION**
→ **DECISION**
→ **ACTION**
→ **OUTCOME**
→ **LEARNING**

Phase 1 is deliberately narrower than the long-term Pyrnova architecture.

The Phase 1 operational customer is a **mid-market U.S. federal contractor**, normally approximately **$50M–$300M annual revenue**, with meaningful federal exposure, multiple programs or pursuits, adjacent capabilities, and insufficient analyst capacity to monitor relevant external change continuously.

Pyrnova does not win Phase 1 by having the largest procurement database, the fastest global event feed, the largest proprietary research corpus, the prettiest graph, or the most AI.

It wins if it repeatedly tells a customer:

1. something materially changed;
2. the change matters specifically to that customer;
3. the reasoning is supported by evidence;
4. Pyrnova found, connected or interpreted something the customer's existing workflow did not adequately provide;
5. the intelligence changes investigation, prioritization, decision or action;
6. Pyrnova remembers what happened afterward.

The primary Phase 1 surface is **Material Changes**.

Material Changes is a decision surface, not an alert firehose.

The healthy default is approximately **5–12 validated customer Material Changes per week**, ordinarily **0–3 per working day**, with only approximately **1–3 per week requiring material customer action**. Quiet days are acceptable. Noise is not.

The canonical Phase 1 workflow is:

**LOGIN**
→ **WHAT CHANGED SINCE I LAST LOOKED?**
→ **TRIAGE**
→ **WHY DOES IT MATTER TO US?**
→ **VERIFY EVIDENCE**
→ **INVESTIGATE**
→ **MONITOR / DISMISS / ACT**
→ **RECORD OUTCOME**
→ **RETURN WHEN NEW CHANGE OCCURS**

Search, company intelligence and program intelligence support this workflow. They do not replace it.

Pyrnova's initial competitive strategy is not immediate replacement of GovWin, GovTribe or another incumbent. It is:

**AUGMENT**
→ **ATTENTION DISPLACEMENT**
→ **WORKFLOW DISPLACEMENT**
→ **BUDGET DISPLACEMENT**
→ **SYSTEM-OF-INTELLIGENCE POSITION**

The initial commercial posture is a **premium mid-market intelligence product**, not low-cost procurement software.

Canonical initial pricing:

- Design pilot list/target: **$12,500**
- Pilot duration: **10 weeks**
- Founder-authorized pilot floor: **$10,000**
- Payment: **50% at signature, 50% at midpoint**
- Annual conversion credit: **50% of pilot fee**, if the annual agreement is executed within 30 days of pilot completion
- Initial annual target ACV: **$48,000**
- Ordinary annual floor: **$36,000**
- Initial stretch ACV: **$72,000**
- Pricing unit: **licensed organizational scope**, not alert volume, searches or raw usage

Phase 1 has **one product**, not a SKU zoo.

M22-A through M22-F are closed and remain closed. Research does not justify reopening them.

The current priority is:

**DESIGN-CUSTOMER DATA-VOLUME + LIVE-OPERATIONS READINESS.**

That phase closes only when Pyrnova can sustain real, fresh, customer-specific intelligence from existing sources, survive unattended operation, expose source health and failure honestly, preserve temporal truth, recover safely, and record the evidence needed to falsify its own commercial thesis.

The binding scope rule remains:

**A capability enters the current phase only when required for correctness, safety, architecture, current acceptance or demonstrated Phase 1 decision value.**

Otherwise:

**DOCUMENT → ROADMAP → DEFER.**

## 2. PRODUCT DEFINITION

### AUTHORITATIVE DEFINITION

Pyrnova is:

**A continuously updated and historically auditable intelligence system that models what external change means economically to a specific organization.**

It combines external observations, customer exposure, economic relationships, evidence, temporal truth and accumulated outcomes to identify material opportunities, threats and changes worth investigating.

Its economic product is not data access.

Its economic product is:

- fewer material intelligence blind spots;
- earlier recognition of economically meaningful change;
- customer-specific consequence interpretation;
- better prioritization;
- reduced manual monitoring and research burden;
- evidence-backed investigation;
- institutional memory about decisions and outcomes.

### RELATIONSHIP TO ADJACENT CATEGORIES

**Procurement intelligence:** Phase 1 evidence domain and wedge, not the identity of Pyrnova.

**External event intelligence:** Important input class. Pyrnova adds organization-specific economic consequence.

**Commercial intelligence:** Correct umbrella description, but too broad on its own to describe the product.

**Company intelligence:** Investigation capability and context surface, not the primary product.

**Threat intelligence:** First-class consequence class within Pyrnova.

**Opportunity intelligence:** First-class consequence class within Pyrnova.

**AI research:** AI can assist reasoning and enrichment. Pyrnova is not an AI research assistant.

**Enterprise search:** Search is supporting infrastructure and investigation workflow, not the product thesis.

**Dashboards:** Presentation mechanism, not product identity.

**Knowledge graph:** Architectural technique, not customer proposition.

**CRM:** Not Pyrnova.

**Palantir-style internal operating platform:** Possible distant architectural adjacency, but not Phase 1 category or current commercial battle.

### PYRNOVA IS NOT

Pyrnova is not:

- another GovCon database;
- a generic procurement-search product;
- a generic alert center;
- a generic company-research tool;
- an AI chat product;
- a document-search product;
- a graph explorer;
- a generic enterprise-search product;
- a CRM;
- a global real-time event firehose;
- a Bloomberg replacement;
- an AlphaSense replacement;
- a Dataminr replacement;
- a Palantir replacement;
- a general supply-chain platform in Phase 1.

### PRODUCT IDENTITY TEST

Any proposed feature should answer:

**Does this materially improve Pyrnova's ability to identify, explain, investigate, act upon or learn from economically meaningful external change for a specific organization?**

If not, it does not belong merely because an intelligence platform could contain it.

## 4. CUSTOMER JOB + PRIMARY WORKFLOW

### CANONICAL CUSTOMER JOB

**What materially changed since I last looked, why does it affect my organization specifically, what evidence supports that conclusion, and what should I investigate or do next?**

### TRIGGER

A new or materially changed external fact becomes available that may affect:

- revenue;
- a contract;
- a program;
- a pursuit;
- a customer agency;
- a competitor;
- a partner;
- a capability market;
- an economically connected organization.

### USER

A capture, BD, strategy or intelligence professional responsible for identifying and responding to commercial change.

### DECISION

The user must determine whether to:

- ignore;
- continue monitoring;
- investigate;
- reprioritize;
- escalate;
- change capture strategy;
- initiate another concrete commercial action.

### ACTION

A real action is an attributable decision or step beyond passive interest, including:

- opening a formal investigation;
- initiating or changing a bid/no-bid review;
- contacting an agency, prime, subcontractor or partner;
- reprioritizing a pursuit;
- changing an account plan;
- investigating a competitor;
- modifying proposal or teaming strategy;
- reallocating resources;
- escalating a threat;
- commissioning bounded additional research.

### OUTCOME

The later result of the decision or action, including:

- opportunity pursued or rejected;
- award or loss;
- changed probability;
- contract impact;
- threat mitigated;
- relationship created or lost;
- decision reversed;
- no material effect;
- still unresolved.

Unknown and unresolved outcomes are valid states.

### WORKFLOW AUTHORITY

**Login → new/updated Material Changes → triage → customer consequence → evidence → investigation → lifecycle decision → action → outcome.**

The user should never need to navigate a graph or build a dashboard merely to discover what changed.

## 6. MATERIAL CHANGES AUTHORITY

Material Changes is Pyrnova's primary Phase 1 decision surface.

### QUALIFICATION

A Material Change must be:

1. tied to a specific customer exposure, capability, program, pursuit, agency, entity or economic relationship;
2. based on auditable evidence;
3. new or materially updated from the customer's perspective;
4. economically or strategically meaningful enough to warrant attention;
5. represented with appropriate uncertainty.

Raw source events do not automatically become Material Changes.

### SUPPRESSION

Suppress from the primary feed:

- duplicates;
- unchanged repeated events;
- stale information represented as current;
- broad keyword or agency matches with weak customer connection;
- unsupported consequences;
- low-materiality administrative noise;
- items whose only novelty is that Pyrnova saw another copy of an already-known fact;
- propagation that adds no material customer information.

Suppression does not require destroying the underlying evidence or historical state.

### MONITORING

Use **MONITORING** when:

- customer relevance is plausible or established;
- consequence is not yet sufficiently certain or material for OPPORTUNITY or THREAT;
- evidence is incomplete;
- an early precursor deserves retention;
- no immediate customer action is justified.

Monitoring is a state, not an excuse to fill the feed with low-value alerts.

A monitored situation returns to prominent feed position only when something materially changes.

### OPPORTUNITY

Use **OPPORTUNITY** when evidence supports a material positive commercial consequence for the specific customer.

It must go beyond "something might be available."

Customer relevance and plausible commercial consequence must both exist.

### THREAT

Use **THREAT** when evidence supports a material adverse commercial consequence or exposure for the customer.

Threat does not imply certainty.

Severity and confidence remain separate concepts.

### NEW VS UPDATED

**NEW:** First customer-material manifestation of the intelligence situation.

**UPDATED:** A material change to an existing situation that alters evidence, consequence, confidence, urgency, scope or recommended investigation.

A second source confirming the same unchanged fact may strengthen evidence without necessarily creating a new feed item.

### FRESHNESS

Pyrnova uses two clocks:

1. **Event time:** When the underlying thing happened.
2. **Evidence availability time:** When Pyrnova could first lawfully and practically know it.

An old event newly ingested is **newly available historical intelligence**, not a current event.

Pyrnova must never represent stale evidence as newly occurring reality.

### EVIDENCE AND UNCERTAINTY

Each material assessment should preserve:

- source provenance;
- observation time;
- event time where known;
- source independence;
- contradictions;
- observed facts;
- Pyrnova assessment;
- uncertainty;
- confidence;
- affected entities/relationships;
- propagation basis.

Unknown is a valid result.

### PRIORITY

Priority should reflect:

- customer consequence;
- customer relevance;
- confidence;
- urgency;
- reversibility / time sensitivity;
- magnitude where knowable.

Priority must not be driven by how many source records exist.

### LIFECYCLE

Canonical states conceptually include:

**NEW → REVIEWED → MONITOR / INVESTIGATE / DISMISS / ACT → OUTCOME**

Specific database labels may differ, but semantic history must remain durable.

### DECAY

Feed prominence decays when an item becomes old or inactive.

Historical truth does not decay out of the intelligence corpus merely because feed prominence does.

## 7. LIVE OPERATIONS + FEED AUTHORITY

### HEALTHY FEED

For the initial ICP:

- **5–12 validated Material Changes per customer per week**
- normally **0–3 on a working day**
- median experience roughly **one useful change on an active day**
- approximately **1–3 genuinely action-worthy items per week**

This is a target range, not a quota.

### TOO THIN

Investigate when:

- sustained volume falls below roughly **2 meaningful changes per week** across a 3–4 week period;
- repeated customer checks reveal no useful change;
- several consecutive working days are quiet despite known activity in the customer's monitored environment.

Do not automatically lower materiality.

First determine whether the cause is:

- genuinely quiet environment;
- insufficient monitoring footprint;
- acquisition failure;
- cadence;
- entity resolution;
- relationship coverage;
- opportunity wiring;
- overly strict relevance;
- another identifiable system defect.

### TOO NOISY

Investigate when:

- the feed consistently exceeds roughly **15–20 items per week**;
- users routinely receive more than **3–4 items per day**;
- urgent/high-priority classification occurs more than approximately once per day without extraordinary cause;
- more than roughly **20–25%** of surfaced items are routine, duplicate, irrelevant or commercially useless.

### PASSIVE MONITORING

Background candidate or monitoring state may be much larger than the visible decision feed.

Do not equate internal monitoring count with customer-facing Material Changes.

### CUSTOMER MONITORING FOOTPRINT

Initial default envelope:

- approximately **50–200 monitored objects**;
- **2–6 core agencies**;
- approximately **10–30 important programs/contracts**;
- approximately **5–15 important companies, competitors or partners**;
- customer capabilities and adjacent capability areas.

The actual footprint should be narrow enough to be meaningful and broad enough to detect cross-relationship consequence.

### ACQUISITION MODEL

Canonical model:

**BASELINE / BULK**
→ **DELTA**
→ **TARGETED LIVE**

Baseline establishes state.

Delta maintains ordinary change.

Targeted live acquisition concentrates scarce cadence and API capacity where customer consequence justifies it.

### ARCHIVE MODEL

**ARCHIVE ONCE → REPLAY MANY.**

Raw authoritative evidence should be archived once wherever lawful and practical, then deterministically reused for customer fan-out, replay, testing and historical analysis.

### SOURCE CADENCE

Source cadence should follow:

- actual source update behavior;
- customer consequence;
- rate limits;
- cost;
- expected useful lead-time advantage.

Faster polling is not inherently better.

Representative Phase 1 cadence from the existing-source research:

- SAM: daily;
- USAspending: nightly;
- Federal Register: daily or limited daily passes;
- Regulations.gov watched items: several times per day where justified;
- GovInfo: daily delta plus periodic bulk;
- SEC watched entities: approximately 15–60 minutes where material;
- sanctions / critical watch sources: approximately 30–60 minutes where consequence warrants it;
- forecasts: weekly;
- budget sources: daily or event-driven;
- Grants: daily;
- slower program sources such as SBIR: weekly where appropriate.

These are operational defaults, not immutable architecture.

### FAILURE AND RETRY

Live operations must:

- retry transient failures with bounded backoff;
- respect source rate limits and cooldown;
- quarantine malformed or structurally changed responses;
- retain safe checkpoints/watermarks;
- preserve idempotence;
- continue independent sources during partial degradation;
- never silently advance state after an unverified failed acquisition.

### SOURCE HEALTH

Minimum semantic states:

- HEALTHY
- DEGRADED
- STALE
- FAILED
- RATE-LIMITED
- UNKNOWN

Source failure must be visible internally and must not silently produce false confidence in customer coverage.

### PRE-PILOT BACKFILL

Before Customer #1 exposure:

- perform approximately **90 days of replay where source semantics support it**;
- use deeper source-specific history where needed for state or context;
- establish baseline entity/program/customer relationships;
- label historical intelligence honestly;
- prevent replay from flooding the present feed.

### UNATTENDED OPERATION

Before customer exposure, Pyrnova must complete an **unattended-week test**.

For at least one continuous week the system must operate without routine human rescue and demonstrate:

- acquisition;
- checkpoints;
- retry/cooldown;
- fan-out;
- persistence;
- restart recovery;
- source-health tracking;
- stale detection;
- no duplicate explosion;
- no temporal leakage;
- no tenant leakage;
- no stale-as-current behavior.

Human judgment may handle genuine exceptions.

It must not be required to keep routine operations alive.

### HUMAN QA

Human QA is exception-based.

Prioritize review for:

- first occurrence of a high-impact event family;
- low-confidence/high-consequence assessments;
- conflicting evidence;
- novel relationship chains;
- source anomalies;
- very large economic consequences;
- legal, reputational or safety-sensitive conclusions.

Routine low-risk Material Changes should not depend on manual analyst approval to exist.

## 8. SOURCE + DATA ECONOMICS AUTHORITY

### CORE DOCTRINE

**OWN THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER.**

**RENT ONLY EVIDENCE THAT CANNOT ECONOMICALLY BE RECREATED.**

### BUILD

Build or maintain Pyrnova-controlled capability when:

- authoritative/public upstream evidence exists;
- normalization or relationship logic creates the differentiated value;
- repeated use makes internal capability economic;
- vendor dependence would distort pricing or product semantics;
- durable historical state creates strategic value.

### RENT

Rent evidence when:

- it provides genuinely exclusive or prohibitively expensive-to-recreate information;
- incremental customer decision value is measurable;
- contract economics support the license;
- rights permit the intended customer use and derivative state;
- dependency risk is acceptable.

### ROUTE AROUND

Route around a source or dataset when:

- rights are incompatible;
- cost exceeds customer value;
- vendor terms contaminate the product architecture;
- upstream authoritative alternatives provide enough value;
- Pyrnova would be paying primarily for somebody else's presentation layer.

### NEW SOURCE GATE

A new source family requires a material combination of:

- repeated customer miss;
- existing source structural inability;
- the same need occurring across multiple customers;
- meaningful expected decision value;
- acceptable rights;
- acceptable cost;
- manageable operational/reliability burden.

The following are **not sufficient reasons**:

- competitor has it;
- API exists;
- dataset is interesting;
- an agent discovered it;
- it could be useful someday.

### PREMIUM DATA GATE

Premium data requires:

- recurring customer need;
- demonstrated public/owned-source ceiling;
- measurable incremental intelligence;
- sufficient ACV or separately attributable revenue;
- acceptable derivative and retention rights;
- acceptable provider dependency;
- poor economics for home-grown replacement.

### DERIVATIVE-RIGHTS RULE

Paid or licensed evidence must remain provenance-tagged and logically separable.

Pyrnova must not allow licensed evidence to permanently contaminate global intelligence state if the license does not clearly permit that derived use.

### REPLACEMENT LOOP

For each rented feed:

1. isolate what value the feed contributes;
2. determine whether value comes from exclusive evidence or vendor engineering;
3. identify lawful upstream alternatives;
4. develop parallel capability when economically rational;
5. benchmark;
6. reduce dependency when Pyrnova reaches sufficient decision-value parity.

Strategic autonomy is a commercial objective, not an ideological requirement to recreate every dataset.

**5. SYSTEM-OF-INTELLIGENCE POSITION:** Pyrnova becomes the primary external-change intelligence starting point.

Phase 1 does not require reaching stage 5.

- first available time;
- first customer-delivery time;
- relevance;
- evidence quality;
- consequence quality;
- actionability;
- action taken;
- eventual outcome.

Competitive claims must be produced from this evidence, not anecdote.

## 12. VALIDATION / FALSIFICATION AUTHORITY

### NOVELTY CLASSIFICATION

Each Material Change should receive one primary novelty classification where measurable.

**ALREADY KNOWN**

The customer already knew the substantive fact and its material relevance before Pyrnova's relevant timestamp.

**PARTIALLY KNOWN**

The customer knew the underlying situation, but Pyrnova added a material detail, relationship or consequence.

Do not inflate this into "net-new fact" merely because the exact source was unread.

**NET-NEW FACT**

Pyrnova surfaced a substantive observed fact previously unknown to the customer.

**NET-NEW CONNECTION**

Relevant facts were known, but Pyrnova identified a material relationship or chain the customer had not recognized.

**NET-NEW CONSEQUENCE**

The facts or relationships were known, but Pyrnova identified a material customer-specific economic implication that had not been recognized.

**NET-NEW OPPORTUNITY**

Use as a positive-consequence subtype or flag where Pyrnova identifies a genuinely new actionable commercial opening.

It should not double-count the same item as multiple independent successes.

### FALSE POSITIVE

A false positive is an item that, after review, is materially:

- incorrect;
- irrelevant;
- overstated;
- stale-as-current;
- duplicate;
- unsupported;
- commercially useless despite being represented as material.

A correctly uncertain item is not automatically a false positive.

A relevant item requiring no action is not automatically a false positive.

### INTEREST

Interest means the customer engaged with, saved, monitored or investigated intelligence without making a concrete downstream decision or action.

### ACTION

Action requires a meaningful attributable decision or external/internal step.

Opening a page by itself is not action.

### DISMISSAL

An explicit customer decision that the situation does not merit further monitoring, investigation or action, with reason where practical.

Dismissal is useful training/evaluation information and is not automatically evidence of product failure.

### MISS / MISSED INTELLIGENCE

**Missed Intelligence is a first-class validation concept.**

A MISS occurs when an in-scope material item that the customer reasonably expected Pyrnova to detect within the relevant window was not surfaced appropriately.

Classify misses as:

- source-coverage miss;
- acquisition miss;
- identity-resolution miss;
- relevance miss;
- classification miss;
- temporal miss;
- prioritization/presentation miss.

Misses exist to improve product decisions.

They are not a blame score.

### OUTCOME

Outcome records what ultimately happened after the intelligence and customer response.

Unresolved is valid and should remain unresolved rather than being backfilled with inference.

### DECISION LEAD TIME

Store separately:

- time of underlying event where known;
- first evidence availability;
- Pyrnova first observation;
- Pyrnova first customer delivery;
- incumbent first relevant alert where provable;
- customer independent discovery where known;
- customer decision/action time.

"Early" should mean earlier in a decision-relevant sense, not merely faster retrieval of a source timestamp.

### PILOT SUCCESS

#### STRONG SUCCESS

A pilot is strongly successful when it demonstrates, in combination:

- repeated net-new or materially better-connected intelligence;
- high-priority precision around or above the **80%** benchmark;
- meaningful customer actions attributable to Pyrnova;
- unprompted return usage across most of the pilot;
- measurable reduction in analyst monitoring/research work;
- credible advantage against incumbent workflow;
- willingness to convert at or above the ordinary annual floor;
- human support burden trending toward repeatability.

A useful cohort benchmark is **approximately 15% or more Pyrnova-only / materially earlier / materially net-new intelligence** among validated comparable material intelligence, provided quality remains high.

This is a falsification benchmark, not an alert-production quota.

#### CONDITIONAL SUCCESS

Examples:

- clear value but concentrated in one agency/program/consequence class;
- net-new advantage closer to 10%–15%;
- precision around 70%–79%;
- useful customer action but incomplete repeat habit;
- willingness to pay exists but scope needs narrowing.

Narrowing is permitted.

Indefinite product broadening is not.

#### EXTEND / NARROW

One bounded extension can be justified where:

- the core signal is strong;
- a clearly fixable, narrow issue prevented a fair test;
- the extension has an explicit acceptance criterion.

Do not repeatedly extend a weak pilot until it produces a success story.

#### FAILURE

Serious failure signals include:

- less than roughly 10% of useful findings being meaningfully net-new/better than incumbent workflow;
- high-priority precision below roughly 70%;
- repeated severe false positives;
- little or no meaningful customer action;
- minimal analyst work displaced;
- incumbent remains the first-open workflow for more than roughly 80% of relevant use after the pilot;
- use collapses without founder prompting;
- customer values Pyrnova only as commodity search;
- customer will not pay the ordinary production floor;
- manual analyst work is essential to make Pyrnova useful.

Praise does not override these results.

### FIVE-CUSTOMER FALSIFICATION GATE

Before materially broadening Pyrnova's product universe, require:

1. **At least 5 real design customers.**
2. **Preferably at least 3 using incumbent intelligence tools**, to create credible comparisons.
3. **At least 3 customers willing to convert at $36K+ annual ACV.**
4. **Repeated net-new material intelligence**, not isolated anecdotes.
5. **Meaningful customer actions in at least 3 accounts.**
6. **High-priority precision around or above 80%, with no repeated severe damaging false-positive pattern.**
7. **Unprompted recurring use in at least 3 accounts.**
8. **Opportunity and threat each demonstrate material value somewhere across the cohort.**
9. **Onboarding no longer depends on engineering intervention or bespoke analyst reconstruction.**
10. **Support burden and gross margin are moving toward repeatable software economics.**

One earlier proposed requirement is modified:

A customer pulling Pyrnova into an adjacent domain is **not required to validate the Phase 1 thesis**.

Budget-backed expansion pull is instead required to justify opening that adjacent domain.

## 23. RESEARCH CONTRADICTIONS RESOLVED

### CONTRADICTION 1: FEED VOLUME

**POSITION A:** A daily workflow requires multiple items per day and a larger monitoring stream.

**POSITION B:** High materiality implies only a small number of weekly customer-facing changes.

**RESOLUTION:** The primary Material Changes surface targets approximately **5–12 validated items/week**, normally 0–3/day. Larger passive monitoring/candidate state remains behind the decision surface.

**REASON:** Habit requires recurrence, not manufactured volume. Raw monitoring volume and customer decision value are different quantities.

### CONTRADICTION 2: PILOT PRICE

**POSITION A:** Earlier research supported roughly $5K–$10K or a broad $5K–$15K pilot range.

**POSITION B:** Later commercial work supports a more deliberate premium position.

**RESOLUTION:** **$12.5K target / $10K founder floor / 10 weeks.**

**REASON:** The later dedicated commercial analysis directly tested the pricing architecture and avoids anchoring Pyrnova as commodity procurement software.

### CONTRADICTION 3: PILOT CONVERSION CREDIT

**POSITION A:** 100% pilot credit toward annual purchase.

**POSITION B:** Partial credit preserves the independent value of the pilot while encouraging conversion.

**RESOLUTION:** **50% credit if annual agreement is executed within 30 days.**

**REASON:** The pilot is paid validation and customer value, not a refundable deposit on future software.

### CONTRADICTION 4: INITIAL ANNUAL ACV

**POSITION A:** Broad $20K–$50K working range.

**POSITION B:** Later commercial research supports higher normalized pricing.

**RESOLUTION:** **$48K target, $36K ordinary floor, $72K initial stretch.**

**REASON:** The Phase 1 buyer is purchasing decision-grade customer-specific intelligence, not search access. The floor also protects support and trust economics.

### CONTRADICTION 5: ICP SIZE

**POSITION A:** Approximately $25M–$500M addressable.

**POSITION B:** Approximately $50M–$300M ideal operating band.

**RESOLUTION:** Keep **$50M–$300M as operational ICP**. Treat $25M–$500M as broader addressable range.

**REASON:** Initial cohort consistency is more valuable than artificially increasing TAM.

### CONTRADICTION 6: HUMAN WEEKLY BRIEF

**POSITION A:** Early customers may benefit from founder-written briefs and analyst support.

**POSITION B:** Human analysis can conceal weak software.

**RESOLUTION:** Weekly working sessions are authorized. Routine bespoke intelligence briefs are not part of the software product.

**REASON:** Founder observation is valuable during design. Manually manufacturing customer value invalidates the experiment.

### CONTRADICTION 7: MONITORING IN MAIN FEED

**POSITION A:** Monitoring is a first-class consequence state and should be visible.

**POSITION B:** Monitoring items can swamp decision-worthy intelligence.

**RESOLUTION:** Monitoring remains first-class in the data/lifecycle model, but appears prominently only when new or materially updated.

**REASON:** Preserve uncertainty and precursor history without turning the primary feed into a low-priority alert queue.

### CONTRADICTION 8: MFA BEFORE PILOT

**POSITION A:** Serious enterprise software should require MFA.

**POSITION B:** Full enterprise IAM should not block design validation.

**RESOLUTION:** **Privileged/admin MFA before pilot. Customer MFA before ordinary paid production.**

**REASON:** This protects highest-risk accounts without pretending Phase 1 needs complete enterprise IAM maturity.

### CONTRADICTION 9: DASHBOARDS IN PHASE 1

**POSITION A:** Pyrnova's longer-term workspace concept is strategically important.

**POSITION B:** Dashboard/workspace infrastructure can derail primary-workflow validation.

**RESOLUTION:** Preserve architectural compatibility now. Do not build generalized dashboard/workspace UX in Phase 1.

**REASON:** The future concept survives without becoming current scope.

### CONTRADICTION 10: ONBOARDING DEPTH

**POSITION A:** Rich customer intelligence improves relevance.

**POSITION B:** Requiring confidential documents creates friction, trust burden and bespoke work.

**RESOLUTION:** Structured profile first. Customer documents are conditional expansion.

**REASON:** Pyrnova must prove that customer specificity can work from bounded structured information before asking for more sensitive material.

### CONTRADICTION 11: API ACCESS

**POSITION A:** Enterprise intelligence products benefit from API access.

**POSITION B:** API productization adds support, security and packaging complexity before demand.

**RESOLUTION:** No general Phase 1 API product. Add separately when a real paying integration need exists.

**REASON:** Architecture should permit it, but hypothetical integration is not current decision value.

### CONTRADICTION 12: SOC 2 TIMING

**POSITION A:** Enterprise credibility suggests early certification.

**POSITION B:** Certification before underlying commercial demand and operating controls is expensive theater.

**RESOLUTION:** Not required for design customers. Pursue Type II when target enterprise pipeline makes absence a repeated blocker and underlying controls exist.

**REASON:** Trust follows consequence and evidence.

## 25. DURABLE DECISIONS

### DECISION 1: PRODUCT IDENTITY

**DECISION:** Pyrnova is customer-specific external-change economic intelligence, not procurement search or generic AI research.

**RATIONALE:** Customer-specific consequence is the repeatedly supported value layer.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Product prioritization begins with change → relevance → consequence → evidence → action → outcome.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Repeated paying-customer evidence that another job materially dominates this workflow.

### DECISION 2: PHASE 1 ICP

**DECISION:** Operational ICP remains approximately $50M–$300M U.S. federal contractors.

**RATIONALE:** Narrowness improves product learning and head-to-head validation.

**CONSEQUENCE:** $25M–$500M remains addressable range, not first-cohort targeting.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Five-customer evidence shows persistent value concentrated outside the band.

### DECISION 3: PHASE CONTROL

**DECISION:** Current scope expands only for correctness, safety, architecture, current acceptance or demonstrated Phase 1 decision value.

**RATIONALE:** Pyrnova's largest execution risk is endless substrate and scope expansion.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Valuable ideas can be deferred without being rejected strategically.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** None. This is a standing operating doctrine.

### DECISION 4: CLOSED MILESTONES STAY CLOSED

**DECISION:** M22-A through M22-F are not reopened for research-recommended refinement.

**RATIONALE:** They met their accepted function and test boundaries.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Refinements enter later work only through current acceptance or evidence.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Genuine correctness, safety or architectural defect.

### DECISION 5: MATERIAL CHANGES IS THE PRIMARY SURFACE

**DECISION:** "What changed since I last looked?" governs the Phase 1 UX.

**RATIONALE:** This is the recurring customer job most capable of creating habit and decision value.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Search and dossiers remain supporting investigation surfaces.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Behavioral data shows another surface consistently initiates the valuable workflow.

### DECISION 6: SMALL NAVIGATION

**DECISION:** Top-level Phase 1 navigation is Material Changes, Search and Configuration.

**RATIONALE:** Company/program intelligence is contextual.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Avoid object-silo navigation growth.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Repeated user evidence that a contextual destination is independently primary.

### DECISION 7: FEED QUALITY OVER VOLUME

**DECISION:** Target approximately 5–12 validated Material Changes/customer/week.

**RATIONALE:** Customer attention is scarce and Pyrnova's value is materiality.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Quiet days are acceptable; monitoring does not flood the feed.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Real pilot behavior establishes a materially different sustainable range.

### DECISION 8: LIVE OPERATIONS MODEL

**DECISION:** Baseline/bulk → delta → targeted live, with archive once → replay many and two-clock freshness.

**RATIONALE:** It balances cost, reliability, replay and temporal truth.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Global high-frequency polling is not the default.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Source or customer latency requirements materially change decision value.

### DECISION 9: SOURCE EXPANSION IS MISS-DRIVEN

**DECISION:** New source families require demonstrated coverage/value need plus acceptable rights/economics.

**RATIONALE:** More sources create permanent operational burden.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Competitor coverage lists do not control roadmap.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Repeated customer misses or budget-backed demand.

### DECISION 10: PREMIUM DATA MUST EARN ITS PLACE

**DECISION:** Own intelligence logic; rent only economically unrecreatable evidence.

**RATIONALE:** Protect margin, autonomy and data rights.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Paid feeds remain separable, attributable and benchmarked.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Exclusive evidence demonstrably creates enough customer value to justify dependency.

### DECISION 11: AUGMENT BEFORE REPLACE

**DECISION:** Competitive strategy progresses from augmentation through attention, workflow and budget displacement.

**RATIONALE:** Incumbents are competent and already embedded.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Phase 1 can succeed without immediate GovWin cancellation.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Customers begin replacing incumbents substantially faster than expected.

### DECISION 12: DIFFERENTIATION IS NOT AI

**DECISION:** Consequence, temporal history, customer exposure and outcome learning are differentiated. AI is implementation.

**RATIONALE:** AI features are broadly replicable.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Marketing and product investment cannot rely on model novelty as moat.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** None absent a genuinely proprietary capability with proven customer value.

### DECISION 13: CANONICAL PILOT

**DECISION:** $12.5K, 10 weeks, $10K founder floor, paid.

**RATIONALE:** Tests willingness to pay while remaining reasonable for design validation.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Free or heavily discounted pilots are exceptions.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** First five pilots show systematic price/value mismatch.

### DECISION 14: CANONICAL INITIAL ACV

**DECISION:** $48K target / $36K floor / $72K stretch.

**RATIONALE:** Positions Pyrnova as intelligence rather than commodity search.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Customers unable to support the floor usually fall outside the initial ICP.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Conversion evidence and measured customer value support adjustment.

### DECISION 15: ORGANIZATIONAL-SCOPE PRICING

**DECISION:** Price primarily around licensed organizational intelligence scope.

**RATIONALE:** Seats and usage do not map cleanly to customer economic value.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Ordinary use is not discouraged by metering.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Product usage or enterprise structure creates a better repeatable value unit.

### DECISION 16: ONE PRODUCT

**DECISION:** Phase 1 has one commercial product.

**RATIONALE:** Premature editions add complexity without validated segmentation.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Premium data, API and custom services remain explicit exceptions/add-ons.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Repeated demand supports materially distinct buyer/value packages.

### DECISION 17: MISSED INTELLIGENCE IS FIRST CLASS

**DECISION:** Customer-expected misses are recorded and categorized.

**RATIONALE:** Misses reveal roadmap needs more directly than feature requests.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Expansion and source work can be evidence-led.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** None. Categories may be refined.

### DECISION 18: FIVE-CUSTOMER FALSIFICATION BEFORE BROADENING

**DECISION:** Core thesis must survive five real customers before broad intelligence expansion.

**RATIONALE:** Prevent architecture enthusiasm from substituting for commercial proof.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Adjacent domains remain gated.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Extraordinary customer demand may justify a bounded exception, but not repeal the gate.

### DECISION 19: TRUST IS STAGED

**DECISION:** Design-customer, production, enterprise and high-sensitivity controls are distinct gates.

**RATIONALE:** Reliability should follow consequence without compliance theater.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Privileged MFA/restore/logging are pre-pilot; broader IAM and certifications follow maturity.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Credible customer requirements become earlier sales blockers.

### DECISION 20: HIGH-SENSITIVITY DATA IS PROHIBITED FOR NOW

**DECISION:** No CUI, credentials, internal telemetry, vulnerability or incident artifacts under the ordinary Phase 1 trust boundary.

**RATIONALE:** These materially change security and regulatory consequence.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Future security capabilities cannot quietly expand data handling.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Explicit high-sensitivity trust gate is designed, implemented, tested and authorized.

### DECISION 21: CUSTOMER PRIVATE STATE REMAINS PRIVATE

**DECISION:** Customer confidential and customer-derived strategic state does not enter the global Pyrnova corpus by default.

**RATIONALE:** Customer trust and contractual clarity are strategic assets.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Cross-customer learning requires explicit rights and genuine de-identification.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Specialist counsel and customer contracts establish broader lawful rights.

### DECISION 22: BAD REVENUE IS REFUSED

**DECISION:** Revenue that creates architectural forks, hidden consulting, destructive support or trust-boundary violations is not good revenue.

**RATIONALE:** Early contracts can permanently distort a product.

**CONSEQUENCE:** Founder retains explicit authority to reject commercially harmful deals.

**REVISIT TRIGGER:** Economics or product structure materially changes the cost of satisfying the request.

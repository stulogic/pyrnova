## Purpose and boundaries

The objective is high-value network density and repeat category authority among relevant
professionals. SOCIAL-GROWTH-001 does not reopen product scope, Phase 1 architecture, customer-data
usage, outreach gates, the commercial offer or the Customer #1 methodology.

The primary audience is growth, BD, capture, strategy and intelligence leaders at approximately
$50M–$300M U.S. federal contractors. Secondary audiences are acquisition specialists,
defence/industrial analysts, relevant journalists, consultants and intelligence peers.

Stu provides commercial interpretation, methodology, category judgment and direct professional
conversation. Pyrnova provides inspectable evidence objects, Pyrnova Origins, provenance, AS-OF,
approved product proof and formal research.

The network plan is 50 primary plus 100 secondary targets, with only 12–15 active relationships at a
time. The roster is discovery material, not a qualified first-50-company cohort; revenue, role and
buying fit require verification.

## Operating guidance

- Founder: two posts weekly. Company: one post weekly. Silence is preferred when material is
  insufficient.
- Founder engagement: four to six substantive comments weekly across three short sessions. Company
  comments are appropriate only when Pyrnova adds meaningful evidence, correction or clarification.
- Founder target: approximately 105 minutes weekly after setup. Research-heavy work may reduce
  volume; verification is not weakened to preserve cadence.
- Content bank: 72 distinct topic briefs; progressively develop 36 evidence-complete fallback assets,
  maintain about six weeks of approved coverage and 12 weeks of viable fallback material, and refill
  when either account falls below about three weeks of valid approved coverage.
- Consequence Review: approximately every 6–8 weeks when evidence warrants it. What Was Knowable /
  Outcome Review: quarterly when sufficient observation history exists. A missed edition is preferable
  to weak work.

The content mix is approximate rolling guidance: 50% evergreen method/commercial education, 20%
historical/provenance, 15% current-event consequence analysis, 10% product proof, 4%
research-release interpretation and 1% company news. About one in four slots is replaceable capacity
for genuinely important current analysis; total output does not increase merely because a live event
occurs.

The primary growth loop is **primary-source consequence analysis → professional discussion → owned
evidence record → later outcome / falsification review**. The primary distribution mechanism is useful
founder participation in established professional conversations connected to inspectable owned
evidence. The primary success metric is verified target companies engaging substantively in at least
two distinct weeks inside a rolling 90-day period. Followers, impressions, generic engagement and
broad technology reach are not substitutes. Use a 90-day initial measurement period before scenario
growth assumptions become operating expectations.

X is optional. Newsletter and owned community are deferred.

## Visual and publication doctrine

The previous Canva-versus-Figma owner decision is superseded. Social production is subordinate to the
current PYRNOVA-DESIGN-AUTHORITY-001 visual authority and
inherits the existing Pyrnova design language. Canva Free may be used for lightweight production and
existing Figma may be used where useful; neither is a required product dependency. Do not create a
separate social identity, generic Canva-template aesthetics, secondary design system or visuals
inconsistent with the Pyrnova visual authority. The accepted EPICENTER / Pyrnova visual language is
the quality reference.

Pyrnova should produce a useful analytical object, not content for content's sake. A public
contribution should generally add evidence, consequence, distinction, question or challenge. Do not
use praise-only engagement, engagement bait, manufactured controversy, automatic commenting,
engagement pods, mass connection requests, generic AI commentary, founder lifestyle marketing,
GovCon tips-account behavior or noisy SaaS promotion.

Stu's account supplies interpretation, category reasoning, methodology, professional conversation
and direct engagement. Pyrnova's account supplies durable evidence objects, provenance, Origins,
AS-OF, research and approved product proof. The accounts must create different value and must not post
substantively identical content.

## Disclosure, rights and human control

Be transparent about evidence, reasoning, uncertainty, dates, limitations and corrections. Keep
customer-private context, advantage-sensitive operations, credentials, detailed commercial pipeline,
personal life, unnecessary internal plans and sensitive implementation details private unless
separately authorized. Public availability does not automatically grant publication or reuse rights.
SOURCE-RIGHTS-001 governs public-use rights.

LinkedIn and X remain human engagement surfaces and must not become automated scraped Pyrnova evidence
feeds. Primary analytical claims should prefer approved primary sources. Restricted or premium
sources follow SOURCE-RIGHTS-001. No automation may conflict with that authority.

Human approval is mandatory for analytical publication. AI may assist source organization, drafting,
formatting, duplicate detection, administrative classification and claim maps. AI must not
autonomously approve or publish analytical output.

## Terminology amendment

The current public canonical external-change analytical object is **Pyrnova Origin**; **EPICENTER** is
the customer surface. A Pyrnova Origin is an external change Pyrnova has determined consequential
enough to initiate downstream analysis. Raw source events do not automatically become Origins.
Pyrnova Cascade, Customer Lens, Consequence Path, Evidence Inspector and AS-OF remain compatible
terms.

The accepted terminology amendment supersedes **Material Change** with **Pyrnova Origin** where that
phrase refers to Pyrnova's canonical external-change analytical object. Unrelated ordinary-English
uses of “material change” are not replaced. Legacy runtime identifiers
and historical documentation are preserved; this codification makes no broad terminology rewrite and
does not assert that legacy product wording is the current public naming authority.
